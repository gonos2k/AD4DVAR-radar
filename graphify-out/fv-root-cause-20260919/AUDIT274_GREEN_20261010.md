## Actionable validator/test gap: initial-base row-only refusal

The earlier concern about a post-commit point was too broad: after commit, `current_products` reuses the final-repeat observation and `final_repeat` already constrains the recomputed minimum to the committed theta at the same tolerance. I therefore do not claim a shifted minimum is reachable on that path.

A narrower initial-base case is source-reachable under the existing admission gates. `_run_child_impl` admits fresh initial side gradients when each component matches the pinned predecessor gradients within the P2 budget and the carried-theta F² matches within its own roundoff budget (`examples/weather_scenarios/fv_point_3h_tangent_continuation.py:784-809`). The row-only chain validator later compares the row theta to the carried theta (`lines 1267-1269`), although the coupled factory forms rows at its newly computed `working_theta` (`examples/weather_scenarios/fv_point_3h_tangent_mixing_minimum_probe.py:298-309`; `examples/weather_scenarios/fv_point_3h_tangent_coupled_gn_comparison.py:310-316`).

I reproduced this through the full child implementation using the pinned PR273 control and saved side gradients, with no FV or derivative evaluation. A synthetic common perturbation to the fresh side gradients preserved their jump direction and the carried mixture norm; maximum component drift was `2.90e-15`, within the tighter side's P2 allowance `3.62e-15`. The fresh F² was unchanged at `0.004490021941881948`, so it passed the initial 128-epsilon F² check. The recomputed theta shifted from `0.4829044955080727` to `0.4829044955080575` (`1.52e-14`), exceeding the validator's `1.37e-14` budget. A synthetic complete row-parity refusal then produced a completed child with 24 row receipts, zero commits, and `chain_closed=false`. The exact production row-parity failure was not regenerated; this establishes the closure mismatch for a refusal at that source point.

The existing parity-refusal regression holds the toy minimum fixed at the carried theta (`tests/test_fv_point_3h_tangent_coupled_gn_resume.py:22-24, 159-178`), so it misses this initial-base case. Fix direction: compare terminal row theta to the same point's recorded `base_mixing_minimum.working_theta_star` (and validate that receipt), then add an initial-base synthetic case with a P2/F²-admissible gradient drift. This concerns classification of a complete row-only refusal; it does not invalidate the saved accepted prefix or explain the actual RSS interruption, whose tail had only one completed row.

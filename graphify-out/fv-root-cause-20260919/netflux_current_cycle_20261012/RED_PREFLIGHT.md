## Limits and remaining checks

- The arithmetic change is a floating-point reassociation, not an algebraically exact transformation in FP64. At extreme scales, `(incoming - outgoing)` can lose cancellation differently or overflow even when separate terms are finite; division by very small area and multiplication by large `dt` also remain scale-sensitive. The admitted fixed case is the supported execution domain for this cycle; this audit does not establish uniform behavior for arbitrary scale, dtype, or boundary data. Existing input checks and affected tests remain important.
- The archived single-point prototype is diagnostic evidence only: it showed material reduction of the common-face support residual at control 556 alongside small changes in J/gradient. It does not identify a universal root cause or establish true-gradient accuracy, convergence, candidate acceptance, forecast accuracy, or benefit over other controls.
- The new plan/runtime checks and preflight test evidence are in place, but final combined verification, the guarded one-cycle run, post-result provenance/metric audit, and final GREEN/RED result review are still pending in `TASK_CHECKLIST.md`. Do not present the preflight as a completed production optimization result.

## Lifetime findings

The row code builds one `torch.func.vjp` pullback per side in `residual_jacobian_rows` ([precondition helper](../../examples/weather_scenarios/fv_point_3h_tangent_precondition_comparison.py:212)). It calls the pullback for each of twelve scalar rows, then deletes the local `pullback` reference and returns the residual vector and a stacked 12×26 row matrix. The caller detaches the residual vector and retains the matrices only through residual parity checks and the one 12-row Woodbury solve ([coupled factory](../../examples/weather_scenarios/fv_point_3h_tangent_coupled_gn_comparison.py:291)). The row callback writes the full child receipt both before each VJP and after each completed row ([counted row wrapper](../../examples/weather_scenarios/fv_point_3h_tangent_precondition_comparison.py:393)). At a `started` callback the reusable pullback already exists and remains live until its row loop returns.

There is no saved evidence that an autograd graph from the completed VJPs or HVPs persists across points. The VJP helper explicitly drops its pullback before return; the coupled solver returns a direction and JSON-compatible audit values; and each current HVP is computed by a local `grad`/`jvp` callback and reduced to a saved call receipt. The current-point cache is reset by `model_state.clear()` before fresh products are installed ([continuation callbacks](../../examples/weather_scenarios/fv_point_3h_tangent_continuation.py:829)). On successful commit, the closure cache replaces its previous observed point. Python locals in `bounded_continuation` do retain aliases to the prior loop’s model/spec and trial receipts until overwritten, and the accepted iteration history intentionally remains live, but static inspection found no previous-point VJP pullback, dense operator, or full control Hessian retained by the GN model closure. These facts narrow the search; they do not prove that the active pullback caused the RSS crossing.

The strongest directly measurable contributor is repeated full-record serialization during row callbacks. `_write` calls `json.dumps(..., indent=2)` for the entire record and then `Path.write_text` ([writer](../../examples/weather_scenarios/fv_point_3h_tangent_continuation.py:565)). The interrupted snapshot’s pretty-printed top-level portions are approximately 61.46 MB for `iterations`, 29.37 MB for `comparison_progress`, 2.24 MB for `last_confirmed_closure`, and 1.98 MB for `initial_branch_trace`. `comparison_progress.model_comparisons` is exactly equal to the committed first iteration’s `model_comparisons`; after commit, the progress field remains in the record. Thus the same large candidate and branch-trace payload is serialized twice, in addition to the final closure and initial trace. The full pretty-printed record is the measured 102.8 MB. Each of the next point’s row-start and row-complete callbacks reserializes that full object while the side pullback is live. The saved evidence supports serialization as a material transient memory cost, but it cannot attribute the sampled peak to one allocation.

## Candidate next-plan change

Keep the numerical method, 600/660-second limits, 1-GiB cap, and row/HVP/solve budgets unchanged. In the next plan, make the atomic writer stream `JSONEncoder.iterencode` chunks to its temporary file instead of first building one full `json.dumps` string; preserve sorted keys, two-space indentation, the trailing newline, and the atomic replace. Also remove `comparison_progress` after `committed_progress` has copied the completed iteration into `iterations`. The live progress snapshot remains available during an uncommitted iteration, while a committed point remains fully recoverable from `iterations` and its closure receipt. This removes a verified duplicate from future checkpoints and avoids holding a full serialized string (and its text-encoding buffer) alongside the VJP pullback. It changes checkpoint lifetime and serialization only; it does not alter the GN equations, accepted candidates, source receipt, or resource ceilings.

These are static recommendations for a new reviewed plan. The frozen attempt and its receipts remain unchanged; the RSS cap was reached, and this note does not authorize another launch.

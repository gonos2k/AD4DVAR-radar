# RED design audit: three-step mixing-minimum continuation — 2026-10-10

## Disposition

The one-step math extends pointwise to three accepted commits without changing the solver: at each current control `c_k`, freshly observe both face extensions, compute the unique resolved strict-interior `theta*_k`, construct the same chart-tangent direction, and take two new side HVPs along that direction. Recompute each candidate's theta-star from its actual side gradients, run the existing actual-J and freshly minimized-Psi/F² gates, and commit `(c_{k+1}, theta*_{k+1})` together only after final closure. The next iteration starts from that commit and repeats the whole current-point calculation. Never carry the prior HVP/model across a commit.

I found one concrete integration blocker in the current shared runner: its mixing-minimum parent structural gate requires exactly one iteration (`fv_point_3h_tangent_continuation.py:1322-1362`), so a valid 2–3-step child will be marked failed. The mixing-minimum adapter is also pinned to the earlier `0da57…` baseline and predecessor archive (`fv_point_3h_tangent_mixing_minimum_probe.py:46-60, 70-129, 133+`); the resume plan needs a new base loader that validates the accepted `e801cf…` raw/gzip/run/resource/manifest chain and its source/input/runtime receipts. These are continuation plumbing requirements, not a defect in the saved one-step result or its derivative.

## Chain checks required

For every iteration record `k`, require its base control SHA to equal the initial `e801cf…` SHA for `k=0`, then the preceding accepted commit SHA. Its carried `theta_k` must equal the preceding closed endpoint theta. Recompute `theta*_k` from the preceding endpoint's saved pair of side gradients (for `k=0`, the archived one-step final closure); require the newly observed point's working theta and both current HVP labels to agree with that value within the established FP64 closure tolerance. Require the HVP pair to be fresh, one per side, at this base SHA, with the same direction digest, current selected-face operator/scope, carried theta, and working theta. An accepted iteration must have exactly two completed HVPs. A strict-interior base-theta refusal may have zero HVPs; it must add no commit and preserve the prior confirmed control/theta. A candidate-theta refusal is a rejected line-search candidate, not a reason to clip or overwrite the carried state.

For every accepted row, require one unique accepted nonzero candidate, the actual candidate theta-star from its fresh side gradients, both Armijo checks on actual J and actual minimized merit, face and strict paired endpoint gates, and the independent final-repeat gates. Require final-repeat control/theta/side gradients and branch traces to match the selected proposal. The final child control/theta must equal the last accepted closure, or the original `e801cf…` control/theta if no step committed. If a later iteration refuses, retain all earlier accepted commits but do not count the refused row as a commit. Preserve attempt index, stop/refusal reason, accepted count, and HVP accounting so a partial sequence is not reported as three completed steps.

For the merit chain, calculate each base and endpoint `Psi_k = 0.5 * ||(1-theta*_k)g_- + theta*_k g_+, Q/0.84||²` from the corresponding saved fresh gradients/face value. Report per-step actual `Delta Psi`/F² and J; summarize the total change only across actually committed steps. The current `working_theta`/HVP metadata alone does not independently establish theta-star: use the preceding final-repeat side gradients as the chain anchor (and archive them for the first base). The one-step result's `base_mixing_minimum` is top-level and overwritten by each direction-model construction, so do not treat it as a three-point history; retain/reconstruct each point from the iteration row plus its predecessor closure.

The existing core continuation loop already invokes the direction factory and side HVP callback anew at every accepted point (`fv_point_3h_tangent_continuation.py:420-478`), and records a per-iteration `working_theta`, HVP direction, and final repeat (`:481-495`, `:510+`). This supports the desired protocol; parent validation must validate a sequential chain rather than the current single-row special case. Keep the per-step chart radius at `0.05`; three bounded moves do not imply a cumulative `0.05` radius bound.

## Bounds and limits

Freeze one guarded run with at most three accepted steps, two current HVPs per eligible attempted point (six total), at most 16 candidate trials per point, 300-second internal / 360-second outer time, and 1-GiB sampled RSS. No row VJPs, PCG, dense solve, cached Hessians, or solver replacement. Boundary/unresolved base minima stop before that point's HVP pair; boundary/unresolved candidate minima reject/backtrack without clipping. Any no-commit or partial-commit exit leaves the final state at the last independently closed endpoint.

This is a finite sequence of local accepted merit steps under the same current branch/face endpoint gates. It cannot establish stationarity, curvature, global smoothness, a minimum/root, coupled response, reanalysis, or forecast skill. Compare local model-predicted decrease with actual minimized endpoint decrease at every accepted step; the prior one-step result had a favorable mismatch (`~0.581%` predicted versus `~8.631%` actual F² decrease), so do not infer model accuracy from acceptance.

No source was changed and no FV, seed, production gradient/HVP, test, or guard was run for this design review.

## Repeat-specific RED diagnostic: clean merit refusal is valid

The baseline direction `d=-t` is not guaranteed to descend `Psi`; it is inherited as a fixed policy direction, while the line search independently gates the actual squared-gradient merit. A concrete supported interior example is the face `Q(x,y)=x=0` with `J_-(x,y)=h(y)-x`, `J_+(x,y)=h(y)+x`, and `h(y)=y-y²/2`. At `(0,0)`, the side gradients are `g_-=(-1,1)`, `g_+=(1,1)`, jump `j=(2,0)` is normal to the face, and the unique minimizer is `theta*=1/2`; the mixed tangent gradient is `t=(0,1)` and `d=(0,-1)`. Both side objectives have directional slope `-1`, but both Hessians applied to `d` equal `(0,1)`, giving the envelope slope `g*·Hmix d=+1`. Along every small positive step `alpha`, native `J(0,-alpha)=-alpha-alpha²/2` decreases while `Psi=0.5(1+alpha)²` increases. Refusing the iteration on the existing strict merit-descent gate is therefore correct, even though J descends and theta remains valid/interior.

A zero tangent residual similarly gives no descent direction. Existing model gates mark `merit_descends=false`, and `candidate_alphas` returns an empty list; the continuation keeps the last-confirmed control/theta. This is clean stagnation/refusal for this fixed direction policy, not a defect requiring a new solver or a claim that no other direction could help. The three-step sequence may validly stop after zero to two commits; its status/accounting must report the actual accepted count and preserve the last confirmed endpoint. This is a bounded local diagnostic, not an unconditional three-step success promise.

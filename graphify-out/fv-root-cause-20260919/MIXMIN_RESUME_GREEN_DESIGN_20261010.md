# GREEN design review: three bounded mixing-minimum continuations

**Decision:** the proposed same-policy resume is coherent as one root-owned run with up to three sequential accepted steps. This is a design/provenance review of saved PR271 endpoint arrays only; no FV, seed preparation, HVP, guard, or production run was performed.

The frozen predecessor is the accepted mixing-minimum run under plan SHA `9611b13d3392c198f0a92436c1e9536a6286363a2c64a70a48e5b43f6c480576`. Its terminal endpoint is control `e801cf4651233ec11b2ec98ac9569e411b8e62e68511440e39db1cb9c3872567`, native `J=0.061240252230254005`, carried theta `0.48316874590279574`, and `F²=0.004689202366398875`. The selected trial and final repeat have exactly equal control arrays, both side-gradient arrays, and trace records; each side has 26 saved gradient components and the two trace signatures match. Recomputing the side-gradient mixture minimum from the saved final-repeat arrays gives theta-star `0.4831687459027956` with `F²=0.004689202366398875`; the one-ULP theta representation difference from stored Torch arithmetic is within FP64 rounding. Thus the resume reference theta and fresh working theta-star agree at entry to rounding, while they should still be recorded as distinct fields.

Each of the three repetitions should begin at the exact last-confirmed control/theta pair. Recompute fresh current side gradients, native J, face/pair gate, traces, and the strict-interior theta-star before building that point's tangent direction. Treat any fresh base reweighting as a merit normalization only, not an optimizer step. Each accepted iteration uses exactly two current-point side HVPs and its own bounded line search; candidate points get fresh side gradients and their own theta-star, actual minimized F²/J Armijo, branch-pair and face checks, and a nonzero chart movement. Final closure must repeat both side gradients, native J, theta-star/minimum F², trace, pair/face, fixed-input, source, runtime, and deadline checks at that exact candidate before commit.

The intended cap is one root-owned guard, at most 3 accepted iterations, 6 HVPs total, 0 Jacobian-row VJPs, and at most 16 candidate slots per iteration (48 maximum across three complete searches), with 300-second internal /360-second outer limits and a 1-GiB sampled RSS ceiling. The prior one-step run took 32.10 seconds and sampled 766,574,592 bytes, but that does not bound a 48-slot continuation. The hard deadline must stop further evaluations and preserve the last fully closed step; initial refusal preserves e801 and its carried theta, while a later refusal keeps any earlier accepted step from this resume. Commit control and its candidate theta-star together, and count only a final-closure-confirmed control change as a step.

These repetitions test local progress under the same branch/face and strict-interior domain. They do not test a full-GN arm, establish convergence or a minimum, or support response/forecast claims. Source/pre-edit snapshots and plan pin verification are deferred to the code preflight before execution.
